# Code of Conduct

Laima welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help maintain a respectful, inclusive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with courtesy and respect, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Give constructive feedback about code, documentation, and ideas. Explain disagreements without attacking people.
- Be patient with questions, language differences, and contributors learning new tools.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted interactions.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and work toward resolving disagreements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, or deliberate intimidation.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or advances.
- Publishing private information without permission, including contact details and private correspondence.
- Trolling, repeated personal attacks, or disruptive behavior that prevents others from participating.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project community spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents Laima, such as through an official project account or at an event.

Maintainers and contributors are subject to the same standards.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, Rodrigo Rangel, at [rodrigo@hangell.org](mailto:rodrigo@hangell.org). Use the subject `Laima conduct report` and include:

- What happened and where, with relevant links or screenshots if available.
- Who was involved and when the incident occurred.
- Any immediate concerns or preferred way to follow up.

Share only information needed to assess the incident. Avoid posting personal details or reports in public issues.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as possible. Information may need to be shared with people necessary to investigate or address the concern; complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this code and explain moderation decisions where appropriate. Depending on severity, impact, and repeated behavior, responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Serious incidents may require immediate restrictions without a prior warning. Decisions should consider the available evidence and protect community participation. A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the same private contact, providing relevant context or new evidence.
